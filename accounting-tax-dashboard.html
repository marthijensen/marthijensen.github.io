The Challenge

Financial data is more useful when it tells a clear story.

Financial and tax information can be difficult to interpret when it is spread across separate tables and spreadsheets. Users may need to compare income, deductions, credits, estimated taxes, refunds, and tax outcomes without a single unified reporting view.

This project focused on organizing related financial records into a Power BI dashboard that makes key insights easier to review, filter, and communicate.

The Solution

A Power BI reporting experience.

I designed the report to provide three levels of financial analysis: a high-level executive view, filtered tax-profile detail, and comparative tax insights across filing statuses, states, income ranges, and deduction categories.

Executive Overview

Summarizes total income, deductions, credits, estimated tax, average tax rate, refunds, and amount due.

Tax Profile Detail

Supports interactive filtering and detailed review of financial measures at the client level.

Tax Insights

Highlights patterns in taxable income, credits, deductions, refunds, and tax rates across multiple categories.

Clear Reporting Design

Uses KPI cards, charts, filters, and tables to make financial information easier to understand.

EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

Financial performance at a glance.

The Executive Overview brings together the most important financial and tax metrics so users can quickly understand the overall picture.

  • Total income, deductions, credits, and estimated tax
  • Average tax rate, refund amount, and amount due
  • Deduction components and share of total deductions
  • Income and taxable income by filing status
  • Refunds compared with amount due

My Process

From raw data to an interactive report.

  1. Prepared the data: Organized financial, deduction, credit, and tax-summary data into structured tables.
  2. Built the data model: Connected related tables to support consistent reporting and filtering.
  3. Created core measures: Built measures for income, deductions, credits, estimated tax, refund amount, amount due, and effective tax rate.
  4. Designed the report pages: Created separate views for executive summaries, tax-profile review, and trend analysis.
  5. Formatted for clarity: Used KPI cards, financial formatting, consistent titles, and visual hierarchy to make the report easy to read.

Portfolio Note: This dashboard was created for portfolio and learning purposes using protected, anonymized, or simulated data. It does not provide tax advice and does not include confidential client information.
