The Challenge

Service desk data is more useful when it tells an operational story.

Ticket counts alone do not tell IT leaders where support demand is coming from, whether urgent work receives faster service, or how workload is distributed across technicians and support levels.

This project turns a large IT service desk dataset into a linked Tableau reporting experience that supports both quick executive review and deeper operational exploration.

What I Built

A focused three-page operations reporting experience.

Each dashboard has a distinct role, helping users move from service-desk volume and backlog visibility to root-cause analysis and performance-focused workload review.

Key Findings

What the dashboard revealed.

Weekly demand was stable

Weekly ticket volume stayed near roughly 5,100–5,400 tickets, providing a consistent baseline for staffing and capacity planning.

Backlog visibility matters

Open and in-progress tickets represented the largest active workload, reinforcing the value of status monitoring for operational prioritization.

Demand was broadly distributed

Issue topics, support channels, and support teams were closely balanced, so no single category dominated total service desk volume.

Urgency influenced speed

Critical tickets received the fastest response and resolution, while lower-priority work took longer as expected in a priority-based support process.

My Process

From ticket data to an operational Tableau workbook.

I focused on making the dashboard useful for an IT leader while keeping the calculations, layout, and navigation clear enough for an unfamiliar user to understand quickly.

  1. 01 Profiled data Reviewed ticket fields, dates, priorities, statuses, sources, agents, and support levels.
  2. 02 Created calculations Built calculated fields for ticket counts, open workload, resolution time, and first-response time.
  3. 03 Designed dashboards Structured executive, root-cause, and technician-performance views around operational questions.
  4. 04 Refined usability Added key takeaways, clear labels, consistent layout, and in-dashboard navigation.

Dataset note: This portfolio project uses simulated IT service desk data from the ITSM Dataset on Kaggle. It was selected to demonstrate Tableau dashboard development, calculated fields, service-level analysis, and IT operations reporting without using confidential organizational data.
